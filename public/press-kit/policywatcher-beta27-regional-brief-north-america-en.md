# North America briefing  -  PolicyWatcher Beta 27

**Evidence date:** 1 August 2026
**Use:** technology, privacy, cybersecurity, legal-tech and developer desks
**Language review:** professional English editorial review required before distribution

## Problem

Policy intelligence products can merge source retrieval, change detection and generated interpretation into one output. This makes it difficult to determine which evidence exists, when it was retrieved and what an automated assessment actually establishes.

## Evidence available

- Public evidence pages retain source-specific timestamps and links.
- A publication gate withholds records that have not met configured evidence conditions.
- “Not assessed” remains non-numeric; it is not converted to a zero score.
- Beta 27 adds operational readiness, role-safe verification and privacy-minimised administrative measurement.
- Public APIs, GitHub release history, deterministic Story Packs and citation-ready embeds support technical inspection.

## Available for editorial verification

Live no-account public surfaces, repository, release audit, architecture discussion, 15-minute founder demo, current screenshots and downloadable press assets.

## Not claimed

Exhaustive North American market coverage, legal conclusions, compliance scores, continuous availability, independent security audit, complete AI accuracy, enterprise adoption or comparison superiority over established compliance products.
