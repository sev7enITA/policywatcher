Please consider the enclosed Original Software Publication, “PolicyWatcher:
Evidence-preserving monitoring of online terms and privacy policies,” for
publication in SoftwareX.

PolicyWatcher is open-source research software for retrieving, validating,
versioning, and communicating changes to online terms of service and privacy
policies. Its main contribution is an evidence-preserving workflow that joins a
five-path acquisition cascade, deterministic content validation, archive
freshness checks, provenance-aware version storage, and a shared public-evidence
gate. This design keeps development fixtures, partial retrievals, challenge
pages, and uncertain records out of public pages, APIs, reports, indexes, and
notifications while retaining diagnostic evidence for research.

The manuscript follows the SoftwareX Original Software Publication structure
and includes the mandatory code metadata table, two original vector figures,
an illustrative workflow, explicit limitations, and a reproducible dated
operational snapshot. The software is publicly developed on GitHub and is
released under the OSI-approved MIT License. The manuscript and accompanying
research material are available under CC BY 4.0.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration by another journal. The author is the creator and
maintainer of PolicyWatcher and operates its public demonstration service, but
declares no financial interest that could have appeared to influence the work.
No specific grant supported this research. The use of generative AI in
manuscript preparation is disclosed in the manuscript.
