## 150-word biography

Fabrizio Degni is the creator and maintainer of PolicyWatcher, an independent civic-tech project designed to make changes in configured public technology-policy sources easier to inspect, cite and correct. The platform keeps source links, retrieval status, timestamps, evidence gates, data-quality conditions and AI-assisted interpretation visible alongside published observations. Its public resources include a Knowledge layer, methodology, Claim Registry, Data Room, Evidence Packets, versioned Story Packs, release records and reusable press assets. Degni’s work also includes the PALO Framework, an operational framework addressing responsible AI lifecycle governance. PolicyWatcher is developed through a public repository under the stated CC BY 4.0 terms. The current configured scope and analytical framework are documented in the dated Press Kit. Neither the platform nor its creator claims exhaustive market coverage, legal advice, measured compliance, independent validation, certified accessibility or the absence of security vulnerabilities. Interviews should distinguish implemented controls, configured scope and measured external outcomes.
