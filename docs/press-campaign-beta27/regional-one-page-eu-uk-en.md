**Evidence date:** 1 August 2026
**Use:** AI governance, privacy, GRC and enterprise-technology desks
**Language review:** professional English editorial review required before distribution

## Problem

Policy-change monitoring can present an empty result or an unavailable metric as if no issue exists. Editors and practitioners need to distinguish source retrieval, baseline verification, public evidence and subsequent analysis before reusing a result.

## Evidence available

- A five-stage readiness model: Configured → Retrieved → Baseline verified → Public → Analysed.
- Deterministic priorities with severity, cause, timestamp, affected records, impact and one responsible-console destination.
- Public source links, methodology, Claim Registry, dated release records, Story Packs and correction routes.
- Configured inventory of 16 companies across six sectors and a framework of 15 canonical KPIs.
- AI Act Article 50 may be used only as regulatory context; PolicyWatcher does not claim to deliver compliance.

## Available for editorial verification

Live product, release audit, 15-minute walkthrough, current screenshots, downloadable data snapshot, methodology interview and citable assets.

## Not claimed

Exhaustive EU/UK coverage, legal advice, AI Act compliance, independent validation, certified accessibility or security, real-time monitoring, complete source retrieval, or measured usability improvement.
