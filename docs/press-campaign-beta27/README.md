# PolicyWatcher Beta 27 campaign source pack

Release: `3.9.0-beta.27`
Evidence date: `2026-08-01`
Campaign source version: `1.0.0`

This directory contains the controlled source material for the Beta 27 international press campaign. Public product facts remain governed by the live Press Kit and Claim Registry.

## Review state

- English and Italian release copy: founder and final fact-check required before sending.
- French, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese copy: professional draft; **native human linguistic review remains required before external distribution**.
- Regional use requires a real local policy implication, monitored source, case or partner. Translation alone is insufficient.
- No document in this directory establishes legal compliance, exhaustive coverage, independent validation, operational health or measured campaign results.

## Contents

- `pitch-*.md`: master and localized pitch drafts.
- `regional-one-page-*.md`: bounded regional briefs.
- `hard-questions-faq-en.md` and `hard-questions-faq-it.md`: difficult-question response source.
- `spokesperson-sheet-en.md` and `spokesperson-sheet-it.md`: approved biography and interview logistics.
- `claims-freeze-beta27.json` and `.md`: immutable campaign snapshot derived from the public Claim Registry.
- `demo-video-transcript-en.md`, `.srt` and Italian `.srt`: demo narration and subtitles.
- `screenshot-register-beta27.md`: captions, dates, rights and boundaries for five current screenshots.
- `translation-review-register.md`: required human sign-off record.
