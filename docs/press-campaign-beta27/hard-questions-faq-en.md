# PolicyWatcher Beta 27  -  hard questions FAQ

Evidence date: 1 August 2026. Answers must be rechecked against the live Press Kit before use.

## How accurate is PolicyWatcher?

PolicyWatcher does not publish a universal accuracy percentage. Accuracy depends on source availability, retrieval, baseline verification, change detection, analysis and review. Public analytical records must pass the configured evidence gate, but that gate does not prove completeness, legal authority or correctness. A journalist should open the cited source and timestamp before relying on an observation.

## Which sources are monitored?

The system uses a configured inventory of public policy URLs. The current product inventory records 16 companies across six sectors and excludes the WAZE administrative onboarding fixture. This is not exhaustive market or regional coverage. A configured source may be unavailable, suspended or awaiting verification.

## Does it store complete policy texts?

Public knowledge and integration surfaces expose bounded metadata and evidence links. They do not promise to expose every raw policy text, internal log, administrative note or withheld record. The source provider remains the authoritative location for its current document.

## What does AI do?

AI can assist structured interpretation, summaries and KPI-related assessment after source evidence is retrieved. The output is an attention and review aid. It is not a legal conclusion, compliance determination or substitute for reading the source. Missing assessments are “Not assessed”, with no numerical value.

## Is PolicyWatcher compliant with the EU AI Act or other laws?

No compliance claim is made. Regulatory developments may provide context for editorial discussion, but PolicyWatcher does not certify that a user, provider, deployer or monitored company satisfies a legal obligation.

## What personal data does the platform collect?

Public press telemetry records allowlisted aggregate actions such as a package-download intent, Data Room view or citation copy. Beta 27 administrative measurement uses bounded event types and excludes IP addresses, user agents, referrers, emails, account identifiers, query strings, free text and arbitrary metadata. Separate voluntary contact or subscription functions are governed by their stated privacy terms.

## Has the platform passed a security audit?

No independent security certification or “zero vulnerabilities” claim is made. Repository checks, release tests and implemented controls are evidence about development activity, not proof that vulnerabilities are absent. Security issues should be reported through the repository’s documented security channel.

## Is it production-ready?

The platform is labelled Beta. Beta 27 documents operational-readiness controls; it does not establish service levels, continuous availability, production health, adoption or fitness for a specific legal or commercial decision.

## What is the business model?

The current public materials do not present a generally available paid plan or commercial entitlement. Roadmap references to marketplace or SaaS packaging describe possible future work, not an available offer. Current funding, sponsorship, commercial discussions or conflicts should be confirmed directly with the founder before publication.

## Is PolicyWatcher independent?

The public project identifies Fabrizio Degni as creator and maintainer and describes itself as independent civic tech. External articles, institutional contacts and monitored companies are not endorsements or independent validations. Any current financial or organisational relationship relevant to a story must be disclosed and confirmed for that interview.

## Is the repository open source?

The repository and release notes are public and reusable under the stated CC BY 4.0 terms. PolicyWatcher does not claim an OSI-approved software licence classification. Journalists should use “public repository under CC BY 4.0” rather than “OSI-certified open source”.

## How are corrections handled?

The Press Kit includes a fact-checking and correction route. A request should identify the cited URL, disputed statement or asset, requested verification and deadline. Corrections should preserve the dated record, explain the affected claim and avoid silently rewriting prior evidence.

## Can a missing or empty result be treated as good news?

No. A missing scan, unavailable module, absent metric or excluded record must remain unavailable, not zero or healthy. An empty administrative priority list or public signal board is not an operational-health assertion.

## Can journalists republish the assets?

Owned press assets may be reused under the attached terms, captions and credit lines. Dimensions, checksum, rights URL and Content Credentials status are recorded in the asset manifest. A checksum establishes file integrity only; it does not prove semantic truth, authorship provenance or endorsement.
