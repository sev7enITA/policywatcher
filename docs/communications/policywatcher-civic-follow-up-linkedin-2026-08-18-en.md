# PolicyWatcher Civic - LinkedIn follow-up post

Publication date: after the Hostinger redeployment that exposes `/en/associations`.

## Main post

PolicyWatcher Civic is now available in English and Italian.

Following the introduction of the global associations vertical, here is a more practical view of what people and organizations can do with it:

- select a region or country and inspect source-backed organization listings;
- filter the public evidence radar by territory, regulatory area and organization type;
- keep a browser-local watchlist and prepare an editable evidence digest;
- submit a new organization or suggest a correction through a reviewable email draft.

The current research snapshot contains 79 organizations across 24 countries, including 41 digital-rights or privacy specialists and eight protection categories.

English workspace:
https://policywatcher.online/en/associations

Italian workspace:
https://policywatcher.online/it/associazioni

The catalog was reviewed on 7 August 2026. It is not exhaustive. Inclusion records a verifiable public source and does not imply partnership, endorsement or legal authority.
