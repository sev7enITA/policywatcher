## PolicyWatcher introduces Civic, a global vertical connecting consumer organizations, digital rights and policy evidence

### The new public workspace covers 79 organizations across 24 countries, eight protection areas, verifiable sources and controlled paths for submissions and corrections

**Milan, 18 August 2026** - PolicyWatcher today introduced **PolicyWatcher Civic**, a global public workspace designed to help consumer organizations, digital-rights advocates, researchers, journalists and citizens navigate organizations and documented changes to online policies.

The initial research snapshot includes 79 organizations across 24 countries. Forty-one records cover digital-rights or privacy specialists. The taxonomy spans eight protection areas: general consumer protection, digital rights, privacy and data, financial services, communications and media, children and families, transport and housing, and food and sustainability.

The directory responds to PolicyWatcher’s global region, country and language context without changing evidence or publication gates. Users can select a country or region, search for an organization, open the public verification source, inspect relevant evidence, maintain browser-local review state and prepare a Markdown digest for human review.

Organizations may submit their own listing through the public contribution form. A valid proposal requires an official HTTPS website and an independent registry or network HTTPS source. Existing cards now include a separate correction action that prepares an email draft with the listing ID, current website and verification source. No submission, acceptance or publication happens automatically.

Directory inclusion records the existence of a verifiable public source. It does not imply affiliation, partnership, endorsement, legal authority, service quality or a legal assessment. The registry is not exhaustive; memberships and statuses may change and are rechecked before updates.

## Core functions

- Global directory contextualized by region and country.
- Eight protection types and bilingual domain search.
- Direct access to the organization website and verification source.
- Copyable, shareable directory views.
- Browser-local watchlist and review states.
- Human-reviewed Markdown digest workflow.
- Separate paths to submit a new organization or correct an existing listing.

## About PolicyWatcher

PolicyWatcher is an evidence-first platform for monitoring, comparing and explaining changes to public policies of digital services. It keeps sources, acquisition state, analysis and editorial decisions distinct while making coverage limits and human accountability explicit.
