# PolicyWatcher Civic - English LinkedIn launch post

## Post ready to publish

**Who helps consumers understand a digital-policy change before it becomes an everyday problem?**

Today I am introducing **PolicyWatcher Civic**: a global PolicyWatcher vertical connecting consumer organizations, digital-rights groups and verifiable public sources to an evidence-first policy radar.

The first research snapshot covers:

- 79 organizations across 24 countries;
- 41 digital-rights or privacy specialists;
- 8 protection areas, from financial services and communications to children, housing and sustainability.

The value is not just the directory. It is the workflow:

1. choose a region or country;
2. find relevant organizations and open the verification source;
3. filter the policy-evidence radar;
4. keep watchlist and review decisions locally in the browser;
5. prepare a Markdown digest for human review before reuse.

Filtered directory views can be copied and shared. Organizations can also **submit their own listing** by providing an official HTTPS website and an independent registry or network source. If an existing record is inaccurate, every listing now includes a separate **Report a correction** action.

Nothing is sent, accepted or published automatically.

One important boundary: **inclusion does not mean partnership, endorsement, legal authority or a judgment on service quality**. PolicyWatcher Civic helps people find and organize evidence; people remain responsible for the decision.
