Every day, the rules behind our digital lives can change. A clause. A permission. A new use of your data. The question is simple. What changed, and where is the proof?

PolicyWatcher helps people and organisations follow changes in the policies of digital services. Explore the source, compare versions, and understand why a change deserves attention. Clear information, with a path back to the evidence.

Today, PolicyWatcher has two complementary sides. The stable service supports everyday use. Engine Six Preview is a separate, private workspace for testing the next generation. One mission, with a clear boundary between public operation and experimental results.

The new pipeline starts before artificial intelligence. Source contracts check document identity, language, required sections, and the ending of the policy. A successful download is only the beginning. Incomplete or ambiguous material is held for review.

Each capture becomes a traceable artifact. Versions can be compared against preserved evidence. A candidate change requires two fresh observations, at least an hour apart. A retry or a cached response does not become a second confirmation.

Analysis must point to exact passages before and after the change. A separate verifier checks the evidence. High impact or uncertain cases move to human review. In the hosted Preview, automatic public changes and notifications remain disabled.

Behind the interface, durable jobs connect runs, tasks, attempts, and stored artifacts. Checkpoints support recovery. Preview and Compare make the work inspectable, including a comparison of two extractors on the very same captured response.

Research does not stop at a new build. Local tests, hosted checks, source completeness, and observations over time answer different questions. Five hundred policies a day is the scale target. Full qualification at that scale is still in progress.

The public research diary opens the architecture, decision flow, and entity relationships. GitHub records the implementation and its limits. This is continuous engineering: measure, challenge, refine, and show the work.
