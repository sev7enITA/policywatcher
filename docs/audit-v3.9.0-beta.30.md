# PolicyWatcher 3.9.0 Beta 30 audit

Date: 1 August 2026

## Delivered Word surface

- Dedicated Word task pane and sideload manifest with `ReadDocument` permission.
- Explicit selection capture followed by local fixed-taxonomy classification.
- Displayed topic labels and a separate acknowledgement before a bounded public evidence query.
- No arbitrary keyword fallback when a clause does not match the taxonomy.
- Route-specific Office framing and script policy while other portal routes retain clickjacking protection.

## Verification

- Focused tests prove confidential example text does not enter the derived query.
- TypeScript, ESLint, public integration consistency and production-build checks cover the delivered surface.
- The task pane includes unsupported-host, no-selection, zero-result and unavailable states.

## Finalization checks

- Release, changelog, roadmap, feature atlas, release-impact map, Press Kit archive and Pulse timeline identify Beta 30 as the current product release.
- Beta 27 campaign cohorts, claims snapshot and localized packages retain their original release identity; package metadata separately records the current product release at generation time.
- Privacy, Security, Confidence Methodology and `llms.txt` state the public-agent and local Word-processing boundaries.
- Generated provider schemas, Microsoft manifests and the Office XML manifest are included in the deployment source package and remain subject to provider-side validation.

## Residual boundary

The manifest still requires controlled Word-host validation and tenant approval. Topic matching can be incomplete or imprecise. The feature maps public evidence and does not verify, approve or legally assess a contract.
