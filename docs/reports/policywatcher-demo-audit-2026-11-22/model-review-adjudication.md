## Accepted challenges

- Four of six synthetic incompleteness mutations still satisfy the source contract. Anchor presence does not establish clause/occurrence/table coverage. Keep this as a qualification blocker and a red reference, not an aggregate parser accuracy percentage.
- The 500-scope SSH exercise is different from scheduled hosted-worker operation. The follow-up subset is a different run; do not sum successes across runs.
- Restored evidence hashes establish bounded persistence; they do not prove uninterrupted availability, semantic entailment, deterministic replay under crashes or batch natural termination.
- Shared-node CPU/RAM are not account allocations. Renderer HTTP readiness is not rendering success, throughput or coverage.
- Hosted private r3 is missing runtime renderer/provider/pair configuration and has only one source contract. Native app reviews do not configure the pipeline.
- iOS compilation and container launch are distinct from Safari extension operation, signing and App Store availability.
