If a service says it “never uses your data for training”, a search for “training” will still flag the sentence. To understand what changed, I want to see the clause, the previous version and the source.

That’s the idea behind PolicyWatcher’s new bilingual Policy Briefing. Choose a service and a topic, see which words matched, then open the published comparison. If you review suppliers, you can also save a responsible role, a due date and the review status in your browser. Those notes are left out of exports by default.

I’ve also published English and Italian press packs with a one-page guide and a screenshot of the live Briefing. Thank you to Economy Magazine and Fortune Italia for covering the project this week.
