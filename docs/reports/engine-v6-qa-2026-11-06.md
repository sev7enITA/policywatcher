No substitutions were made after failures. These are metadata candidates, not 500 independently approved policy identities. OTA selectors, executable filters and engine were not run; retained third-party declarations remain separate local reference material.

## Contract counterexamples and recovery

`scripts/engine-completeness-falsifiers.ts` runs seven synthetic document-level cases. The complete control passes. Missing required heading and missing terminal controls are rejected. **Four incomplete documents still pass `contract_satisfied`: missing middle clause, lost repeated occurrence, missing table row and empty required section.** Each case retains a known reference and occurrence counts. This demonstrates that title/heading/tail/length/language checks alone do not establish independent completeness. It is not a demonstration that the live HTML extractor omitted those clauses or that a false alert was published.

The existing recovery probe injected 500 expired synthetic leases across two runs and four real local processes. It passed in **1.062 seconds**: 250 requeued, 250 terminally failed, all 500 attempts marked expired, no live leases, zero policy changes/outbox entries, DB integrity OK. This measures deterministic recovery from injected expiry, not 500 real sources or natural wall-clock expiry.

Local verification: **120 engine tests passed**, scoped ESLint passed and full TypeScript checking passed. These results belong to the local candidate, not automatically to the distinct hosted artifact.

## Independent model reviews and adjudication

Gemini **4 Argon / High** was used through the existing web session. Its review supported occurrence-aware reference annotations and highlighted PDF provenance. Several assertions were rejected: private Preview was incorrectly called production, zero human reviews were conflated with zero validator execution, and it proposed remediating the immutable failed temporal window. The correction was sent back; Gemini acknowledged the distinctions in a retained addendum. Its proposed lead/tail witnesses and minimum section lengths still cannot prove that every middle clause exists. No model recommendation changes historical evidence or grants completeness approval.

GLM **5.3 / Max** completed a read-only ZCode review of the pinned candidate source and the test log. Its eight findings distinguish static analysis from supplied execution measurements. The following adjudication preserves disagreements:

| GLM finding | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Missing contracts reduce the eligible denominator | Confirmed by source reading and the hosted 1/4 capture. Applying the single Apple contract to unrelated sources would merely test URL mismatch, so that proposed replay is rejected. |
| PDF evidence loses original bytes and usable structure | Supported for the inspected conversion path and AWS evidence. General claims that every possible PDF can never pass are too broad; PDF source qualification remains open. |
| Static direct content can hide rendered changes | Plausible acquisition/contract-design risk, not an observed false unchanged result. Requires paired rendered/direct references and audited transport requirements. |
| Boundary contracts do not prove middle-clause coverage | Confirmed by four of six incomplete cases; the seventh case is the complete control. Rejecting all seven would be wrong. The additional claim of stray table-text loss was tested: the marker was retained, so this particular counterexample does not reproduce a loss. |
| Distinct models do not ensure independent errors | Supported as a limitation: the runtime pair shares a provider and uses resolved model-ID inequality as a minimum gate. A second provider alone is not an accuracy guarantee. |
| Large diffs exhaust output budgets | Caps exist and should remain explicit; a universal claim that 100 claims cannot fit or that all full rewrites fail is not demonstrated. A measured size distribution and safe holds are required. |
| Full 500/day evidence is absent | Confirmed. Reject the suggestion to extrapolate a smaller end-to-end day into a qualification of 500 real policies. |
| Storage growth and legacy-run lockout need qualification | Retention/restore remains open. The legacy-run hold is deliberate and explicitly returns a recovery-required error; automatic takeover without establishing that the old worker stopped would weaken the fence. |

GLM's proposed 95% coverage and 98% agreement thresholds were not adopted as product acceptance criteria. Fifty reviewed cases with zero fabrication would still not prove population-wide accuracy. Its findings are review input, not execution or certification.

OpenRouter used only explicitly free endpoints. Nemotron 3.5 Super VL returned a truncated review (generation `gen-1793945226-d0YBVKRVqQ8LKFRlv8sg`, confirmed cost zero); Gemma 4 returned 429, and Nemotron 3 Ultra failed at the connector transport with no generation receipt. Nemotron 3.5 Lightning completed (generation `gen-1793946499-cmFCbTEURMtT99nLefyU`, confirmed cost zero), but its acceptance criteria were rejected: a verbatim quotation does not prove correct interpretation, a cross-source 10% similarity threshold has no basis, a 500 +/- 2 job denominator is unacceptable, and scanning for literal synthetic marker names does not detect genuine omissions. These were advisory reviews, not a benchmark of production model accuracy. No paid endpoint, top-up or service purchase was used.
